# Decision frontier

**Use when:** a plan depends on unresolved decisions and their order is unclear.

**Published method:** pinned [grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md) works the available decision frontier in rounds. [5 Agent Skills I Use Every Day](https://www.aihero.dev/5-agent-skills-i-use-every-day) explains resolving decision dependencies before planning.

**Published attribution:** the article explicitly credits Frederick P. Brooks's *The Design of Design* for the design tree.

**Local inference:** investigate environment-answerable facts first, then surface the smallest human decision whose answer changes the next move. A two-hour, well-scoped task is not enough evidence for a huge decision-map workflow.

**Installed authority:** read `grilling` for the interview discipline. Keep wrapper and flow selection with `ask-matt`; read the relevant installed wrapper before recommending it.

**Observable done:** the current dependency is resolved or its next decision-maker and required evidence are named. Downstream speculation does not substitute for that decision.
