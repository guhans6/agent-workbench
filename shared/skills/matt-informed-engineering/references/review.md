# Review

**Use when:** a diff seems plausible but its standards or requested outcome remain unproven.

**Published method:** pinned [code-review](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/49dd158d1076134a641b33efb035946536778336/skills/engineering/code-review/SKILL.md) assesses Standards and Spec separately against a fixed point. Findings carry evidence; repository standards override the heuristic smell baseline.

**Published attribution:** the [official guide](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-code-review) names chapter 3 of Martin Fowler's *Refactoring* as its smell baseline. Smells remain judgment calls, not automatic violations.

**Local inference:** keep “matches the requested behavior” separate from “fits design rules.” Resolve a finding with a cited requirement or observable check; a succession of clean model opinions is not independent proof.

**Installed authority:** read `code-review` and applicable local review/routing limits. This rationale does not change the review's invocation, delegation, or approval contract.

**Observable done:** the fixed point and requirements are identified, findings on both axes cite evidence, and unresolved findings or an unavailable specification are reported honestly.
